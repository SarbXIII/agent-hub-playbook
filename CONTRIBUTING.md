# Contributing

This is a playbook, not a product. There is no support commitment.

- **Corrections and field reports are welcome.** If a rule failed in your context, say what happened, the mechanism, and what you changed — same format as `lessons/`.
- **Keep it generic.** No personal data, machine paths, client names, costs tied to a person, or identifiable incidents.
- **Everything is tested before it is published.** Proposals without evidence can be discussed, but they do not enter the specification.
- **Style:** answer first, process after; short sections; no marketing language.

Open a discussion for ideas. For a concrete change, send the diff and the evidence that it works.
